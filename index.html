Who We Are

Roots & Shoots Rwanda is part of a global movement inspired by Dr. Jane Goodall, the renowned primatologist, conservationist, and humanitarian. Established in Rwanda in 2021, the organisation brings young people and communities together to make a positive difference for people, animals, and the environment.

Our Story

The movement began in 1991, when Jane Goodall met with a group of young people in Tanzania who wanted to make a difference in their communities. From that simple gathering grew a global movement built on compassion, hope, and the belief that every individual can change the world.

Inspired by her vision, Roots & Shoots Rwanda continues this legacy through:

  • Environmental education
  • Wildlife conservation
  • Animal welfare
  • Tree planting
  • Community engagement

We believe that meaningful change begins when people care enough to take action.
