Who We Are

Roots & Shoots Rwanda is part of a global movement inspired by Dr. Jane Goodall, the renowned primatologist, conservationist and humanitarian. Established in Rwanda in 2021, the organisation brings young people and communities together to make a positive difference for people, animals and the environment.

The movement began in 1991, when Jane Goodall met with a group of young people in Tanzania who wanted to make a difference in their communities. From that simple gathering grew a global movement built on compassion, hope and the belief that every individual can change the world.

Our Work

Inspired by her vision, Roots & Shoots Rwanda continues this legacy through environmental education, wildlife conservation, animal welfare, tree planting and community engagement. We believe that meaningful change begins when people care enough to take action.

Planted in Honour of Dr. Jane Goodall

This tree is planted in honour of Dr. Jane Goodall and the extraordinary legacy she leaves behind. As it grows, may it remind us that hope is something we nurture through our everyday actions.

Her journey continues through every young person inspired to care, every animal protected, every community empowered and every tree planted.

Her hope took root.
Our actions keep it growing.
